# Nether World

### A world where the Nether is everything.

What if the Nether wasn't just another dimension you visited on your adventures — but the entire world you called home?

**Nether World** turns Minecraft into a completely different survival experience. Forget starting in a peaceful forest, gathering wood and building your first shelter. Here, your journey begins in the heart of the Nether, surrounded by lava seas, crimson forests, dangerous creatures and ancient ruins.

There is no ordinary Overworld to escape to. This is your world now.

---

## A World Without an Overworld

Explore the Nether as your only home. Travel through its biomes, discover hidden places, gather resources and build your own safe haven among the dangers.

- **Five Nether biomes** to explore, each with its own atmosphere and challenges.
- **No playable Overworld** — your adventure stays in the Nether.
- **A safe starting point** gives you a place to begin your journey.
- **A protected obsidian platform** is restored when you return from death or from the End.
- **Natural progression** built around the resources and possibilities of the Nether.

Every expedition matters. Every discovery brings you closer to mastering this hostile world.

## Ancient Strongholds Beneath the Ash

The Nether holds secrets of its own.

Deep within its landscapes, ancient Strongholds can be found — mysterious underground structures hiding one of the most important steps in your journey.

- Discover naturally generated Strongholds throughout the Nether.
- Follow the Eyes of Ender to uncover their locations.
- Find the End Portal and prepare for the journey beyond your world.
- Earn the classic **Eye Spy** advancement by discovering a Stronghold.

These are real, naturally generated Strongholds — not decorative ruins or artificial structures.

## The End Is Still Within Reach

Your journey doesn't end in the Nether.

Find a Stronghold, activate its portal and enter the End. Face the Ender Dragon, survive the final battle and complete your adventure.

When you return, the Nether will still be your home. Your journey continues from the same protected starting point.

## Build, Craft and Survive

A different world deserves a different approach to survival.

Nether World adapts familiar crafting possibilities to the materials available in your new home.

- Netherrack can be used in stone-related crafting and tool recipes.
- Familiar mechanisms and building components remain accessible.
- Build your own bases, bridges and fortresses using the resources around you.

You don't need to abandon the things that make Minecraft creative. You just need to learn how to use them in a world made of fire.

## Designed to Feel Like Minecraft

Nether World isn't a separate game or a replacement for Minecraft. It transforms the way you experience survival while keeping the familiar foundations of the game.

- Vanilla world presets remain available.
- The Nether World experience is selected separately when creating a new world.
- Familiar structures, items, achievements and progression remain part of the adventure.
- The End remains accessible, giving your journey a meaningful goal.

---

## How to Play

1. Install **Minecraft: Java Edition**.
2. Install **Fabric Loader** and **Fabric API**.
3. Place the Nether World mod and Fabric API in your `mods` folder.
4. Launch Minecraft using your Fabric profile.
5. Create a **new world**.
6. Select **Nether World** in the world preset selection.
7. Start your adventure!

**Important:** Choose the Nether World preset when creating a new world. Existing worlds are not automatically transformed.
Build your fortress above a lava sea. Explore the crimson forests. Search for forgotten Strongholds. Challenge the Ender Dragon.

**Welcome to Nether World.**
